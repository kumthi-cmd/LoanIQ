LoanIQ Labs — Neural Lab logo kit (v1)
======================================

WHICH FILE TO USE
  LoanIQ app header (48 px high)      -> logo/loaniq-labs_horizontal_dark-bg_48px.svg
  Website header on a dark/navy page  -> logo/loaniq-labs_horizontal_dark-bg.svg
  Website header on a white page      -> logo/loaniq-labs_horizontal_light-bg.svg
  LinkedIn / slides / quick share     -> logo/loaniq-labs_horizontal_on-navy.png (navy background baked in)
  Square spaces (About page, posters) -> logo/loaniq-labs_stacked_*.svg
  Avatar (GitHub, LinkedIn page)      -> icon/app-icon-1024.png
  Single colour (watermarks, print)   -> logo/loaniq-labs_horizontal_mono-*.svg
  Just the symbol                     -> logo/loaniq-labs_mark_*.svg

  Prefer the .svg files on the web: they stay sharp at any size.
  The lettering is outlined, so they look the same on any computer.
  The .png files have transparent backgrounds (except "on-navy" and the Apple icon).

FAVICON — paste into the <head> of every page, with the icon/ folder at the site root
  <link rel="icon" href="/icon/favicon.ico" sizes="any">
  <link rel="icon" href="/icon/favicon.svg" type="image/svg+xml">
  <link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="/icon/apple-touch-icon.png">

COLOURS
  Lab Navy     #0A1628   backgrounds, ink
  Neural Gold  #F2B705   brain, "IQ" on dark
  Amber        #D49A00   "IQ" on white (more contrast than gold)
  Flask Blue   #2563EB -> #1A3F9A (liquid gradient)
  Slate        #9FB3CC   "LABS" on dark   |   #51647D on white
  Ink Navy     #0B2545   flask + "Loan" on white

TYPE
  Montserrat Bold (LoanIQ) + Montserrat Regular, tracked wide (LABS)
  Inter for body text and UI
  Both are free Google Fonts.

HEADER LOGO, 48 PX (added 2026-11-19)
  logo/loaniq-labs_horizontal_dark-bg_48px.svg is drawn for exactly 48 px high
  (144 x 48), so show it at that height. It makes "LABS" legible at header size:
  bold, brighter (#D3DEEC) and followed by a gold rule, instead of thin and tracked wide.
  It differs from the v1 kit above: Space Grotesk Bold lettering (outlined) instead of
  Montserrat, and a simpler flask and brain with thicker strokes.
  The other files are still v1.

CLEAR SPACE
  Keep empty space around the logo at least the width of the flask's neck.
  Don't stretch, recolour, or put the full-colour logo on busy photos (use mono-white there).
